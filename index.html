Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I have attic rats?

Listen for scratching or scurrying along the ceiling line, especially at dusk and dawn. Other signs include chewed insulation, dark rub marks along beams, droppings near the attic entry, and a musty smell. If you notice any of these, it's worth having a professional confirm before it spreads.

Are roof rats dangerous to my health or home?

Yes. Roof rats can chew through electrical wiring (a fire risk), contaminate insulation, and carry parasites and pathogens that affect indoor air quality. The longer they nest, the more damage and risk builds up, which is why early removal matters.

How do you get rid of rats safely and humanely?

We use humane trapping methods matched to the situation, remove the animals, and then seal every entry point we find during inspection. This two-step approach — removal plus exclusion — is what actually keeps rats from coming back.

What if a rat dies inside my walls or attic?

We offer dedicated dead animal removal — locating the source of the odor, removing the carcass, and sanitizing the area. This is a common follow-up need and one we handle as part of the same visit whenever possible.

Is professional rat pest control actually affordable?

We price jobs based on the actual scope of entry points and infestation size, not a flat inflated rate, which keeps our rat pest control genuinely affordable. You'll get a clear quote before any work begins — no surprise charges after the fact.
